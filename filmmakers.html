The Station

What AI Television is

AI Television is a TV station for AI films. The films come from independent AI filmmakers, not from the station. Our job is to present them the way a station should.

Our host, Lindsay, introduces every film. Premieres run live on YouTube, with live chat, and every premiere gets an encore later the same night, so your audience can be there whenever they watch.

The schedule is public, and it is real: Upcoming premiere dates.

Premiere Night

What a premiere includes

  • A night of your own. Your film premieres at a scheduled time, presented as an event, not dropped into a feed.
  • The full station treatment. A host introduction from Lindsay and the station's presentation around your film. Your film itself airs exactly as you delivered it. We do not cut, re-edit, or alter it.
  • Promotion. Your premiere is promoted on the station's channel and social pages, and featured in Coming Attractions, our weekly guide to what is on the station.
  • Poster artwork. You receive the poster artwork made for your premiere, yours to keep and share.
  • Your name up front. You are credited, with your links, everywhere your film appears on the station.

SERIES NIGHT

Making a series? Episodic work has its own home on Series Night. Episodes premiere as a series, presented as one body of work, and new episodes join the night as they arrive.

The Fit

What fits the station

Finished films. Features, shorts, and series, in any genre. The station is entertainment first.

Over three minutes, a film gets a Premiere Night of its own. Shorter films are grouped into a Shorts show, presented the same way.

Dates are set once your film is in hand and checked, together, in writing, and never more than a month ahead.

The Terms

Plain terms, in plain words

  • Non-exclusive. The license you grant is non-exclusive. You can show your film anywhere else, before, during, and after your premiere.
  • Yours. You keep full ownership of your film. A premiere never transfers your rights to the station.
  • Your uploads stay untouched. Your own copies of your film, on your own channels, are never affected by a premiere here.
  • It stays up, and it comes down when you ask. Your film remains on the station after its premiere, for as long as you want it there. If you ask us to take it down, it comes down.
  • In writing first. The full terms come to you in writing before anything airs, and nothing airs until you have seen them.
  • On revenue. The station is not monetized yet. When a premiere earns revenue here, you share in it, and the full terms come to you in writing before anything airs.
Said Plainly

A young station, and we say so

We are a young station, and we would rather tell you that plainly than dress it up. Our audience is still growing, and we will not promise you a crowd we cannot promise.

What we offer is the premiere itself, done properly: a real night, a real presentation, and a station that treats your film like it matters. That is the offer, and we stand behind it.
